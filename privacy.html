Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Space Shorts Engine ("the app") is a personal, single-user tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Pengartian. It is not offered to other users. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how it is handled.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube Data API (scope youtube.force-ssl), for the owner's own channel only: uploading videos, reading and updating the privacy status of those videos, and listing the channel's own uploads.
  • YouTube Analytics API (scope yt-analytics.readonly): reading view statistics for the channel's own videos.

The app does not access any other Google data and does not access data belonging to anyone other than the channel owner.

How the data is used

Only to publish the owner's videos and to measure how they perform, so the app can choose better clips. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties, and not used to train machine-learning models.

Where the data is stored

Everything stays on the owner's own computer: the Google sign-in token is kept in the macOS Keychain, and video records and view statistics are kept in a local database. Nothing is sent to any other server, other than requests to the Google APIs listed above.

Keeping and deleting data

  • View statistics from the YouTube Analytics API are kept for at most 36 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Other data returned by the YouTube APIs (for example responses recorded in the app's activity log) is kept for at most 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • The app's record of the owner's own videos (title, video ID, publish time) is kept while the app is in use.

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page, myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local database deletes everything it stored. Questions or deletion requests: pengartian1@gmail.com.

Cookies and device storage

The app uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and no third party collects information through it. It stores the Google sign-in token and its records only on the owner's own computer, as described above.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
