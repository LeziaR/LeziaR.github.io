Space Shorts Engine

Space Shorts Engine is a personal tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Pengartian. It makes short vertical videos from NASA public-domain footage — with captions and credits — and publishes them to that one channel.

It is not offered to the public: it runs on the owner's own computer and only connects to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Footage is courtesy of NASA's public Image and Video Library. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.

The videos appear on Pengartian on YouTube.
